Talks & Reach

Douglas Fritz’s talks and posters, newest first, with where each was hosted, its research pillars and links to any public event page, slides, poster or recording.

On this page, reach means the documented places and institutions that hosted these talks, and the public materials that go with them. An in-person talk is listed at its event location. A virtual talk is listed at its host’s location and marked Virtual: it does not imply travel. The summary counts presentations, countries, cities, hosts, public links and years only. It makes no claim about audience size or impact.

Search looks in titles, authors, hosts, locations, presentation types and topic tags. The pillar filter matches a talk's primary or secondary pillar. Foundational work, earlier research that is not central to the current focus, is hidden unless you include it.
