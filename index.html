Douglas Fritz

Translational physician-scientist for public health

MD/PhD researcher and NIH Oxford–Cambridge Scholar, based in Cambridge, UK

I work at the intersection of climate change, infectious disease, medicine and health-professional education. My doctoral research combines experimental virology, epidemiology and evolutionary modelling, and I help translate climate-health evidence into education, communication and action across health-professional networks.
